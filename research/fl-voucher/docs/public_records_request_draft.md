**Subject:** Public records request under Chapter 119, Florida Statutes: private school and scholarship participation data, 2017-18 to 2024-25

Dear Records Custodian,

Under Chapter 119, Florida Statutes, I request electronic copies of the following records held by the Department of Education (Office of Independent Education and Parental Choice). I am a [graduate student / researcher] at [institution] studying how the 2023 expansion of Florida's scholarship programs affected private school tuition. Please send machine-readable files (CSV or Excel) where possible.

**1. Private school annual survey data, school level, school years 2017-18 through 2024-25.**
For each private school and year: school code, school name, address and county/district, religious/non-profit status, grade levels served, enrollment by grade (PK-12), date of survey submission, and any open/closed status.

**2. Historical versions of the Directory of Private Schools.**
The annual directory file for each school year 2017-18 through 2024-25, in the same format as the current "Download School Contact List" file, including the scholarship participation indicators as they stood in each year. (The Directory's download form as it appeared in June 2019 offered these fields: district, non-profit status, accreditation, Florida Tax Credit Scholarship participant, McKay Scholarship participant, religious status, denomination, and total enrollment. Please provide those fields for each year.)

**3. Scholarship participation by private school and year, 2017-18 through 2024-25.**
For each participating private school and year, for the Florida Tax Credit Scholarship (FTC), the Family Empowerment Scholarship for Educational Options (FES-EO), and the Family Empowerment Scholarship for Students with Unique Abilities (FES-UA) (and predecessor Gardiner/McKay programs if kept in the same system): school code, number of funded students, total dollars funded, and the years the school was approved to participate.

**4. Scholarship participation by district and year**, the same measures as item 3 aggregated by district, for FES-EO for each year (the quarterly reports I could find cover FTC only).

**5. Tuition or fee information.**
If the Department holds tuition or fee schedules for private schools (for example collected through the annual survey, school applications to participate in the scholarship programs, or scholarship funding organization reporting), please provide them by school and year. If none exists, please say so.

Student-level data are not requested. Counts below 11 may be suppressed, consistent with your published reports. If any part of this request is unclear, too broad, or would be costly, please contact me so we can narrow it rather than deny it. If any record is withheld, please cite the specific statutory exemption. Please provide an estimate of any fees before incurring them.
