# Public records requests: drafts (not sent)

Two separate requests, so that a delay or denial on one does not hold up the other.
Fill in the bracketed fields before sending. Save the sent email and its date.

**Where to send** (confirmed on the Department's Public Records Requests page, 2026-10-08):
- Email: PRR@fldoe.org
- Phone: 850-245-9759
- Mail: Florida Department of Education, Attn.: Public Records, 325 W. Gaines Street, Suite 1544, Tallahassee, FL 32399

The page also says the Department does not maintain student records; neither request asks for any.

Optional first step: the Office of Independent Education and Parental Choice has an "Email us" form on its site and could say whether these files exist. That form is not a formal records request.

## Request A: private school annual survey and directory data (send first)

**Subject:** Public records request, Chapter 119: private school annual survey and directory data, school years 2017-18 to 2024-25

Dear Records Custodian,

Under Chapter 119, Florida Statutes, I request electronic copies of the following records held by the Department of Education (Office of Independent Education and Parental Choice). I am a [graduate student / researcher] at [institution] studying how the 2023 expansion of Florida's scholarship programs affected private school tuition. Please send machine-readable files (CSV or Excel) where possible.

**1. Private school annual survey data, school level, school years 2017-18 through 2024-25.**
For each private school and year: school code, school name, address and county/district, religious and non-profit status, grade levels served, enrollment by grade (PK-12), date of survey submission, and any open/closed status.

**2. Historical versions of the Directory of Private Schools.**
The directory file for each school year 2017-18 through 2024-25, in the same format as the current "Download School Contact List" file, with the scholarship participation indicators as they stood in each year. (The Directory's download form as it appeared in June 2019 offered these fields: district, non-profit status, accreditation, Florida Tax Credit Scholarship participant, McKay Scholarship participant, religious status, denomination, and total enrollment. Please provide those fields for each year.)

If a year's file does not exist in the form described, please send whatever school-level records for that year you do hold, or tell me that none exist.

Student-level data are not requested. If any part of this request is unclear, too broad, or would be costly, please contact me so we can narrow it rather than deny it. If any record is withheld, please cite the specific statutory exemption. Please provide an estimate of any fees before incurring them.

Thank you,
[Name]

## Request B: scholarship participation and tuition (send separately)

**Subject:** Public records request, Chapter 119: scholarship participation by school and district, 2017-18 to 2024-25

Dear Records Custodian,

Under Chapter 119, Florida Statutes, I request electronic copies of the following records held by the Department of Education. I am a [graduate student / researcher] at [institution] studying how the 2023 expansion of Florida's scholarship programs affected private school tuition. Please send machine-readable files (CSV or Excel) where possible.

**1. Scholarship participation by private school and year, 2017-18 through 2024-25.**
For each participating private school and year, for the Florida Tax Credit Scholarship (FTC), the Family Empowerment Scholarship for Educational Options (FES-EO), and the Family Empowerment Scholarship for Students with Unique Abilities (FES-UA), and the predecessor Gardiner and McKay programs if kept in the same system: school code, number of funded students, total dollars funded, and the years the school was approved to participate.

**2. Scholarship participation by district and year.**
The same measures aggregated by district, for FES-EO for each year. The quarterly reports I could find cover FTC only.

**3. Tuition or fee information, if held.**
If the Department holds tuition or fee schedules for private schools (for example from the annual survey, school applications to participate in the scholarship programs, or scholarship funding organization reporting), please provide them by school and year. If none exists, please say so.

Student-level data are not requested. Counts below 11 may be suppressed, consistent with your published reports. If any part of this request is unclear, too broad, or would be costly, please contact me so we can narrow it rather than deny it. If any record is withheld, please cite the specific statutory exemption. Please provide an estimate of any fees before incurring them.

Thank you,
[Name]
[Institution, email, phone]
